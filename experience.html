Experience

I have hands-on experience building, configuring, and troubleshooting desktop and laptop computer systems, including hardware installation, Windows and Linux OS setup, and basic network connectivity issues. This includes diagnosing boot failures, component faults, and software-related performance problems using systematic troubleshooting methods.

In addition, I have real-world experience working with electrical systems on motorcycles in a dealership environment, working at a BMW Motorrad and Indian motorcycle dealership. I regularly used manufacturer-specific diagnostic software, scan tools, and electrical testing equipment to identify faults in sensors, wiring, and control systems. This required interpreting diagnostic data, verifying symptoms, and confirming repairs under time constraints.

Troubleshooting Approach

The following is the troubleshooting methodology I developed through hands-on diagnostic work, which directly transfers to IT, cybersecurity and system administration roles:

  • Identify and verify the reported issue
  • Collect diagnostic data using appropriate software or tools
  • Analyze system data, fault codes, and logs
  • Narrow the scope and identify likely root causes
  • Test potential solutions from most to least probable
  • Verify resolution through follow-up testing and diagnostics
