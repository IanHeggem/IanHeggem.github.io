Hobbies

I have many hobbies but of them all, the biggest are motorcycles, computers/tech, and gaming.

Motorcycles

My biggest hobby is motorcycles. I have been riding motorcycles since July of 2022. My dad got me into riding by paying for me to take the Motorcycle Safety Foundation safety course to learn how to ride as my High School graduation gift, and ever since then I have fallen in love.


I have owned the following motorcycles in my riding career:

  • 2022 Kawasaki Ninja 400 KRT Edition
  • 2005 Yamaha YZF-R6 Raven Edition
  • 2023 BMW F750GS Sport
  • 2023 BMW R1250GS Trophy Edition

From track days, to long distance trips, to riding off-road through the cascades in the Pacific Northwest I have done it all!

Computers & Gaming

Another one of my hobbies is building computers and gaming. I have been gaming ever since I was a kid, and started my hobby of building desktop computers in 2020 when I was stuck at home at the beginning of the COVID-19 pandemic. I started watching a bunch of tech videos on YouTube, did a bunch of research, and built my first gaming PC. Since then, I have built a handful of desktop computers and upgraded a handful of laptop components as well. I have also familliarized myself with the process of installing both Windows and Linux based operating systems.
