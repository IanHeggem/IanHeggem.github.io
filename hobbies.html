Hobbies

I have many hobbies but of them all, the biggest are motorcycles, computers/tech, and gaming.

Motorcycles

My biggest hobby is motorcycles. I have been riding since July 2022, when my dad paid for me to take the Motorcycle Safety Foundation course as my High School graduation gift. Originally, I wasn't really interested in motorcycles but after taking the MSF course I went out and bought a bike the next day and have been hooked ever since. From track days, to long distance trips, to riding off-road through the Cascades in the Pacific Northwest, I have done it all!

Computers & Gaming

Another big hobby is building computers and gaming. I've been gaming since I was a kid, and started building desktop PCs in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic. After watching several tech videos on YouTube and doing lots of research, I built my first gaming PC, and I have built and helped others build several more since. I've also familiarized myself with the installation process of both Windows and Linux operating systems.
